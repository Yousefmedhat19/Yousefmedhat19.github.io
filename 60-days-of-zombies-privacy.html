Who we are

“60 Days of Zombies” (the “Game”) is a mobile game published on Google Play by its independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the Game.

Information the Game itself collects

None. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

Your progress (city, day, inventory, settings and language) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any server we run. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage, deletes it.

The Game does not sell anything for real money.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: an ad between days, and optional “watch an ad for a reward” offers that you choose to start. To serve, measure and protect these ads from fraud, Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • Device and app information (model, operating system, language, the Game’s version)
  • Ad interactions (which ads were shown, taps) and diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the Game shows Google’s consent message before any ads, and you can choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s Settings (Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to delete. For data held by Google, use your Google account and device settings.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains cartoon horror and zombie combat.

Security

Game data stays in the Game’s private storage on your device. Information sent to Google’s ad services travels over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
